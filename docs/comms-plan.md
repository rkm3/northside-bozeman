## Social media

### Instagram — primary

- **Handle:** [@northsidebozeman](https://www.instagram.com/northsidebozeman/)
- **Role:** official public account for the business group and strolls.
- **On the website:** not linked yet (footer and any feed carousel wait until the account is
  active enough that a visitor won't land on a stale profile).
- **Content fit:** stroll dates and maps, member spotlights, district history, event day
  atmosphere. Prefer real photos over graphics; follow imagery rules in `DESIGN.md`.

### Instagram — legacy

- **Handle:** [@bozemannorthsidebuzz](https://www.instagram.com/bozemannorthsidebuzz/)
- **Role:** older neighborhood buzz account. May be worth reviving later (archive posts, redirect
  bio, or merge into the primary handle).
- **On the website:** do **not** link in the footer, carousel, or contact page.

### Other platforms

No Facebook, Threads, TikTok, or newsletter is official yet. If someone creates one, add it here
before linking it from the site.

**Decide:** who owns the Instagram login (and a backup), posting cadence, and whether member
businesses can be tagged/repost-featured without a formal approval step.

## What we announce, and where

| Message | Members (Google Group) | Instagram | Website |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stroll date / time confirmed | Yes — `[Stroll]` | Yes | Yes — event page + home spotlight |
| Call for stroll participants / map deadline | Yes | Optional teaser | Optional on event page |
| New or updated business listing | Optional FYI | Optional spotlight | Yes — after maintainer deploys |
| Brand or naming decisions | Yes — `[Brand]` | Only after decided | Update `DESIGN.md` / site copy |
| Meeting notes / internal logistics | Yes | No | No |

Strolls are the group's main public beats: **Summer Stroll** (July) and **Winter Stroll**
(December). Plan Instagram and site updates around those dates first; fill quieter months with
member and district posts if capacity allows.

## Website as a channel

The site's public jobs (from the build doc):

1. Live directory of Northside businesses (`/map`, place pages).
2. Home for the two strolls (`/events`, home spotlight).

Supporting pages: `/about`, `/contact`, `/member` (brand, add-a-business, contacts).

Do **not** put private member emails, the Google Group address (unless the group chooses to make
it public), or the member directory Sheet on the public site.

When Instagram is ready for the site:

1. Add a footer link to [@northsidebozeman](https://www.instagram.com/northsidebozeman/).
2. Optionally add a feed/carousel on the home or about page.
3. Point `/contact` (or the footer) at the same handle so email and social stay consistent.
