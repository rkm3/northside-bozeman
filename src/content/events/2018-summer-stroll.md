Friday, June 1st, 2018. Local shops opened their doors for discounts, drinks, and dinner, with a Bozeman Flea pop-up on the block and a food truck roundup from Engine 8.

The poster — a watercolor map of Wallace, Cottonwood, Peach, and Rouse — was one of the early stroll maps that sent people from studio to bakery to brewery. We kept this photo from the old [@bozemannorthsidebuzz](https://www.instagram.com/bozemannorthsidebuzz/) account.
